About Habesha Market

Habesha Market is a fictional Ethiopian marketplace that showcases authentic local products. Our goal is to connect customers with quality Ethiopian goods, including traditional clothing, coffee, spices, and handcrafted items while supporting local businesses and artisans.

Location: Bole, Addis Ababa
